UK Economic Statistics

Accurate, sourced figures for exam use. Data from the World Bank, ONS, Bank of England, and IMF.

Year Inflation (%) GDP Growth (%) Unemployment (%) Interest Rate (%)

Sources: Inflation — World Bank / IMF (CPI annual average). GDP Growth — IMF / ONS (constant prices, annual %). Unemployment — ONS / OECD (annual average, aged 15+). Interest Rate — Bank of England official Bank Rate (average where multiple changes occurred in a year; 3-month interbank rate used as proxy for years prior to 2009).

Year GBP / USD GBP / EUR GBP / JPY

Sources: GBP/USD — Federal Reserve H.10 (annual average of daily noon rates). GBP/EUR — European Central Bank / World Bank (annual average). GBP/JPY — Bank of England / IMF (annual average, indicative). Exchange rates are annual averages unless otherwise stated. Note: A-level economics frequently uses the "USD per GBP" convention. For example, a rate of 1.25 means £1 buys $1.25.

Year Budget Deficit / Surplus (% GDP) Government Debt (% GDP)

Sources: Budget deficit — IMF World Economic Outlook (general government net lending/borrowing, % of nominal GDP). Government debt — IMF (general government gross debt, % of GDP). A negative deficit figure indicates borrowing; a positive figure would indicate a surplus.
